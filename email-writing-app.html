💡 Informal Email Tips 7 helpful reminders · expand when needed
  • Write as if you're chatting to your friend — warm, natural, relaxed.
  • Use contractions: I'm, don't, we'll, it's, can't.
  • Cover all 3 bullet points and develop each one with detail.
  • Organise with clear paragraphs: opening, one per bullet, closing.
  • Start with a friendly greeting and end with a question or plan to meet up.
  • Use linking phrases that sound natural: anyway, before anything else, that's why.
  • Don't sound like a formal report. Avoid furthermore, consequently.

Reread your sentences for range, accuracy and organisation (Cambridge language criteria: 9 marks). No automatic grades.

Cambridge guidance on errors

A high-scoring email can still contain occasional mistakes when communication remains clear. Aim for effective writing, not unnecessary complicated expressions.
