# Review Writing Academy — educational release specification

Source: user-supplied 0511-review-writing-app-brief(1).md, adopted 24 November 2026. This specification records the implementation requirements; source links are teacher references, not independently verified in this change.

## Exam and teaching
Cambridge IGCSE ESL 0511 Paper 1 Reading and Writing, Exercise 6: review is one possible text type alongside article, report or essay. 120–160 words, continuous prose, clear paragraphs, formal or semi-formal as appropriate to audience. Content /6 and Language /9 assessed independently against official bands; no mechanical point-per-feature deductions. Title, four paragraphs, reason for visiting, praise and criticism are flexible teaching choices. Speech bubbles inspire ideas; students develop useful ideas in their own words and add their own observations.

Every teaching, writing and revision screen: compact reminder plus expandable guide. Introduce → Describe → Evaluate → Recommend. Introduce specific subject and why visited/watched/read/tried if relevant; describe concrete features; evaluate with specific supporting details (description and evaluation may share a paragraph); recommend to a suitable audience with reason. Ask “What specifically made me think that?” after judgements. Toolkit: highlight feature, explain benefit, introduce drawback, contrast (Although/However), specific recommendation.

## Student journey and release gates
Learn (reorder model and identify moves with explanations) → Improve (write a more specific opinion, multiple reasonable responses accepted) → Plan (audience, reason, two features, recommendation) → Write (task accessible, live answer-only word count, continuous prose) → Review and revise (retain original, compare versions, substantive change). Bronze prompts/starters, Silver planning questions, Gold independent task and compact reminder. Planning notes must not be inserted automatically into answer. Draft recovery must survive refresh, changing guidance and failed feedback; associate drafts with authenticated student and task. Use existing Hub auth and server checker with limits; optional AI, practice estimates only, evidence-based strength and next step, quoted excerpts from actual draft. Self-check: correct subject/audience, supported opinion, suitable recommendation and reason, clear paragraphs and 120–160 words. No checker outage blocks revision.

Seed guided restaurant task and Willow Kitchen model, independent community sports event task (original content in review-guided-v1.js). Keep stable IDs, scenario, audience, publication, optional ideas and annotations. Treat student text as untrusted. Keep official past-paper questions separate. Preserve existing submissions and teacher visibility.

Acceptance: normal student Hub entry; full journey and recovered draft; exam reminder at every stage; reason for visiting; counts at 119/120/160/161 and whitespace; guided model within range; original tasks; improvement feedback distinguishes vague from supported opinions without pretending to grade open writing; desktop and phone verification. Existing staff preview is not a release.
