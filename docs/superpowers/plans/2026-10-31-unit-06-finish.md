- Scope is Vocabulary Builder Unit 6 only.

- Thirty distinct Unit 6 target occurrences across the three forms.
- Preserve isolated Unit 6 occurrence IDs where relevant: `u6_12_evaluate_6`, `u6_31_struggle_6`, `u6_40_pressure_6`, `u6_46_achievement_6`.
- British spellings remain `prioritise`, `summarise`, `memorise`.
- Do not invent new mechanics or change scoring/progression.

## Review Focus

- Every authored question target resolves to an actual Unit 6 occurrence.
- No accidental fallback to Unit 1 or another unit.
- Diagnostics do not reveal answers.
- All MCQs have four unique choices and exactly one correct answer.
- The Unit 6 cover matches the established wide unit-cover family and renders from the standard asset path.
