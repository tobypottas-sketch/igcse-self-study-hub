# R2.1 critical defect resolutions

[Question standards](../question-standards.md) apply to all future work. The [R2.0 report](final-report.md) and its individual records preserve the original evidence. The exact revised sources, prompts, option text and per-option feedback are versioned together in [the staff curriculum module](../../../src/writing/content/review-critical-r2-1.js).

| Activity | Original defect | Revised decision and review against alternatives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pedagogy-q-019` | Start times alone could not justify the recommendation. | **Choose the demonstration.** It runs 4:30–5:00, so the five-minute walk gets readers to the stop at 5:05. The practical session finishes at 5:40, after the 5:30 bus. Leaving at 5:25 would allow the walk but fail the requirement to finish the robot. The Master recommendation now needs all constraints. |
| `pedagogy-extra-q-31` | Opposite recommendations were equally defensible for unspecified readers. | **Recommend the film for its believable characters and acknowledge the slow middle.** The club must choose this week and names believable characters as its main priority. Choosing the only faster alternative gives up that priority. Advising viewers about slow pacing throughout generalises a problem limited to the middle. The Silver task still weighs evidence against a reader need. |
| `pedagogy-extra-q-27` | Service time alone could not establish a complete visit. | **Recommend going only if seven minutes is enough to eat and service is as fast as observed.** Twenty minutes less two four-minute walks and five minutes at the café leaves seven. Eleven omits the return walk and twelve omits collection. Feedback teaches the inference without promising future service. |
| `pedagogy-extra-q-30` | Shared benches did not establish reduced hands-on practice, and two alternatives gave plausible evaluations. | **Tools are included, but each beginner gets ten minutes to practise.** Only one person can work at a time, each has a stated turn, and readers want both equipment access and practice. Each other option illustrates the consequence of overlooking one of those priorities. |
| `pedagogy-observation-006` | The two café sentences supplied time but no location. | **Both versions tell readers when it is noisy.** Lunch and midday provide timing. Neither version identifies the cause or tells readers how often it happens across days. The feedback explicitly models keeping that detail when rephrasing. |

The editorial answer pass checked the supplied facts and both alternatives for each prompt, then compared its conclusions with the stored keys and feedback. It did not involve learners or a separate human reviewer.

## Compatibility and verification

Each item retains its activity ID, moves to version 2 and uses new option IDs. Only staff preview mounts select the revised items. The browser restores unaffected decisions and writing. Version-1 options reopen the changed question; affected Practise and Master completion are recalculated while drafts are preserved. The Vocabulary item replaces one legacy length gate with the existing recognition widget. Its old text is retained in local state and old completion does not satisfy the new check.

The regression suite checks the five answer identities and version boundaries, preservation of neighbouring decisions and drafts, and preview access. The simulated browser suite exercises the live renderer with a mock staff account. It covers the five revised activities, two preview routes and desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile widths. Live school-account and learner testing remain outstanding.
