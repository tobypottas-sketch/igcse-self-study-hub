## Progression and examples

| Stage | Previous task | Refined task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Write a short response about what changed in a seat description. | Identify the physical support described. Feedback distinguishes support, general comfort, softness and space. |
| Explore, Beyond the Stars | Replace “opening scene”. | Keep the claim that the film interested the writer from the start without changing it to a claim about speed. “Opening scene” remains available as a sentence note. |
| Explore, Summer Sound | Replace “featuring young musicians”. | Choose a natural opening that preserves the writer’s reason for going. Hearing unfamiliar bands, supporting familiar bands and finding the organisers are distinct motivations. |
| Explore, The Lost City | Replace a phrase about vivid description and explain an abstract difference. | Compare what it means to picture a setting. The optional classroom-display rewrite has an example and three self-review checks. |
| Bronze | Pick the only natural option in a question about good value. | Compare low price, worthwhile cost and free entry against a ¥50 workshop with included tools and individual help. |
| Silver | Choose an option that criticises crowding without attacking staff. | Help classmates with a short lunch break decide whether they can sit together. The revision must place the ten-minute wait at noon and attach it to finding a table. |
| Gold | Give exhibition advice based on an assumed quieter visiting time. | Keep the observations about the crane, labels and crowding. Advice to check the displays on arrival follows the evidence. The review target is 45–65 words and the comparison unlocks at 30 words. |
| Apply | The café example locates the greeting at the door. | The example states that the greeting happened on arrival. The other workshops still use festival evidence and a targeted recommendation for puzzle-game readers. |
| Master decision | Change the strength of criticism without changing time or place. | Choose a newsletter extract for readers who need captions and may need seating. The selected extract explains the captions, gives actionable seating advice and confines the bench observation to the back room on that visit. |
| Master writing | Combine the editor’s note and review in one example. | Write a separate note and a 35–55-word exhibition review. Retain two signs, low light and the location near the exit. The note sits outside the review word target. |

The answer review checked each alternative against the full source. The register records the defensible answer and the reason each alternative changes the fact, meaning or intended effect. R2.1’s lunchtime Discover decision and R2.2’s moving-performance gap and exhibit comparison remain in place. Gold and Apply continue to use example-led comparison and self-review. The optional Explore writing is also self-reviewed.
