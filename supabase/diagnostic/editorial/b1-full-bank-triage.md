## Cross-level observations

- Many B1 questions use a target word in the stem and a nearly explicit synonym in the key; this may measure recognition but not enough range to separate B1 from A2.
- Polysemy items 023, 107 and 108 are promising for discrimination but must not depend on obscure syntax or artificial sentences.
- Causal inference and background knowledge can contaminate scores; flag 022, 027, 029 and 065 for independent construct review.
- A successful four-option structural check does not establish distractor plausibility or one defensible answer.
- Do not change production question versions, reviews, flags or the disabled student feature until the editorial revision process is approved.
