## Cross-level concerns

- A1 *lend* and A2 *borrow* may be worth retaining as distinct directional concepts, but test redundancy and local dependence.
- A2 *refund* and *exchange* are a promising coherent contrast; preserve their conceptual precision rather than inventing silly distractors.
- The bank contains placeholder rationales such as 'a plausible related action or inference'; these must be replaced by item-specific explanations before any approval.
- The final diagnostic must assess vocabulary ability, not primarily numeracy, grammatical patterns, cultural familiarity or test-wise elimination.
- Keep all items in draft; require independent single-key and CEFR reviews before piloting.
