## Editorial gates

- Check whether students can identify the key by extreme alternatives, discourse format, obvious synonym or common-sense elimination.
- Require exactly one defensible answer, particularly where the prompt asks for “best” evidence or interpretation.
- Test the intended lexical construct rather than subject knowledge, numeracy, fairness judgments or reading length.
- Independently check the provisional CEFR label and item-specific rationale; pilot before making level claims.
- Preserve drafts and versioning until formal review; do not silently update approved or student-facing items.
