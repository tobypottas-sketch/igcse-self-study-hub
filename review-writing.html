What makes a useful review?

01 · Introduce02 · Describe03 · Evaluate04 · Recommend

Give the reader enough context, share balanced opinions with reasons and specific details, then offer a clear recommendation. Adapt the structure to the actual task and audience.

Plan your review

Short notes are enough. Use the prompts to organise ideas; you do not need full sentences yet.

01 · Introduction

What are you reviewing, and what should the reader know first?

02 · Description

Select the most relevant details — don't just list features.

03 · Opinion

Give a judgement and support it.

04 · Recommendation

Decide who would enjoy it and why.
